## Project Description

<br />
**Project Title:** Detection of AI-based Fake Investigation and Tip Videos  
<br />
**Duration:** Jul. 2022 - Feb. 2025  
<br />
**Funding:** Supported by Korean National Police Agency & Ministry of Science and ICT ($2.2M in total)  
<br />
**Role:** Project member (Black Box time removal and detection model development)  
<br />
This project aimed to develop advanced techniques for detecting AI-generated fake videos, particularly those used in fabricated investigations and tip-offs. The research explored multiple directions, including **deepfake detection, video forgery detection, video inpainting, and video-to-video translation**.  
The system was designed to ensure reliability and robustness against emerging generative video manipulation technologies, contributing to the prevention of misinformation and social harm.  
The project was recognized with an **Outstanding Achievement Award in Social Problem-Solving R&D** by the **Minister of the Ministry of Science and ICT**.
